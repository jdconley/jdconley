It's been forever since my last post. I promise I'll do better. I've just been juggling three jobs. ;) But that has changed (more on that soon)!

The last couple of nights I did the same talk at two different user groups. [Sacramento .NET User's Group](http://www.sacnetug.org/) and the [Central California .NET User's Group](http://www.centralcaldotnet.com/). Thank you guys for having me, and not throwing any tomatoes. I think we had a good time at both events. Though, I did take up the whole two hours both times.

The talk was based on [Jon Skeet's](http://www.yoda.arachsys.com/csharp/) upcoming book titled [C# in Depth](http://www.manning.com/skeet) which I had the pleasure of reviewing and providing technical feedback. We went through the evolution of C# from 1.0 to 3.0, explored a bunch of the new features, played [Human LINQ](https://msmvps.com/blogs/jon.skeet/archive/2008/02/14/human-linq.aspx) (hilarious). Oh yeah, it was pointed out to me in the Sacramento group that the word "jumped" should really be "jumps". That's what I get for copying my work! hah! If anybody in the Sacramento area wants to help, I'd like to do it again and video tape it...
